Report abuse

To report illegal content, malware, copyright infringement, or any other content that breaks the Terms of Service, contact us through the address above and include:

  • The share code(s) or link(s) of the content in question;
  • A short description of the problem (what the content is and why it violates the terms);
  • For copyright claims: identification of the copyrighted work and a statement that your report is accurate and made in good faith;
  • A way to reach you if we need to follow up (optional).

We may remove shares that violate the terms and may restrict access for repeat violations. See section 6 of the Terms of Service.

Lost codes and passwords

Shares are anonymous and temporary: there are no accounts, so we cannot look up who created a share. We cannot recover a lost share code or a forgotten password, and we cannot open password-protected content for you. If you know a share’s code and need it removed, include the code in your message and we will consider it.
